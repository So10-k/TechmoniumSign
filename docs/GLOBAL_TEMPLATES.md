# Global template library

Techmonium Sign includes a read-only starter library for common business documents:

- Mutual nondisclosure agreement
- One-way nondisclosure agreement
- Employment offer letter
- Independent contractor agreement
- General services agreement
- Consulting statement of work

Global templates are seeded into `contracts_Template` by a database migration. They are readable by authenticated workspaces but cannot be edited or deleted by tenant users. Selecting **Use** creates a new tenant-owned document, so the sender can fill the commercial details, assign signers, and edit the copy without changing the global source.

The starter agreements are general templates, not legal advice. Users should review completed agreements with qualified counsel for their jurisdiction and circumstances.

## Source and regeneration

Template content and widget coordinates live in:

```text
apps/OpenSignServer/data/globalTemplates.cjs
```

PDF source artifacts are generated by:

```text
scripts/generate-global-templates.cjs
```

The generator uses the repository's installed `pdf-lib` dependency. Regenerate the PDFs after changing template text or coordinates, rebuild the client image, run the focused global-template unit suite, and deploy the migration and client together. The committed PDFs live under `apps/OpenSign/public/templates/` so their stable URLs remain available to documents created from the library.
